ELTiS Practice Test

Grammar
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read some sentences and choose the best answer to complete each sentence.
1. Did the stunning performance make the audience ________?
正解: C. excited
【解説】`make + 目的語(the audience) + 形容詞(excited)` の構文です。観客が「興奮させられた状態」を表すため過去分詞形容詞の excited が入ります。
2. ________ you heard this musical soundtrack before?
正解: C. Have
【解説】現在完了形（経験）の疑問文です。主語 `you` と過去分詞 `heard` に対応する Have が正解です。
3. Many critics believe this musical is ________ of the three.
正解: C. the most beautiful
【解説】3つ以上の比較（of the three）で「最も美しい」を表すため、最上級の the most beautiful が入ります。
4. My brother is planning ________ music composition at the university.
正解: A. to study
【解説】動詞 `plan` は目的語に to不定詞 をとるため、to study が正解です。
5. The 1950s was a time ________ musical theater experienced rapid growth in New York.
正解: A. when
【解説】先行詞が時を表す名詞 `a time` であり、後ろに完全な文が続いているため、関係副詞の when が正解です。
6. The audience saw the famous conductor ________ his hands in the air.
正解: A. waving
【解説】知覚動詞 `see + 目的語 + 現在分詞`（Oが〜しているのを見る）の構文です。動作が進行中であることを表す waving が入ります。
7. ________ that his musical score was accepted, the composer decided to celebrate.
正解: B. Knowing
【解説】「〜と知っていたので」という理由を表す分詞構文です。文頭に現在分詞の Knowing が入ります。
8. If I had a ticket for the Broadway show tonight, I ________ to the theater right now.
正解: C. would go
【解説】If節が `had`（仮定法過去）のため、帰結節には `助動詞の過去形(would) + 動詞の原形` である would go が正解です。
Reading and Comprehension
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read and answer questions about some passages.

1Many American composers have created works that reflect the energy of theater and film. Leonard Bernstein (1918–1990), one of the most charismatic musicians of the twentieth century, wrote memorable music that brought stories to life on both stage and screen. His most famous musical, West Side Story, adapted Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet to the streets of New York City. Through rhythmic jazz beats and delicate melodies, Bernstein captured the passion, conflict, and heartbreak of urban youth. Listeners can feel the tension in dramatic dance numbers as well as the gentle tenderness in romantic songs like "Tonight."

10The theatrical success of legendary composers was matched by other legendary musicians who transformed American entertainment. Just as Bernstein brought classical sophistication to Broadway, George Gershwin blended classical structure with jazz in Rhapsody in Blue and the opera Porgy and Bess. Later in the century, film composer John Williams drew inspiration from these rich traditions to create grand orchestral scores for movies such as Star Wars and Jaws. This powerful combination of symphonic music and dramatic storytelling became a defining feature of modern American culture.

18However, Bernstein and other composers who wrote music for the stage and screen did not just aim to entertain. They used their melodies to tell audiences deeper human truths. Music brought emotional depth to serious social issues, urban life, and human relationships. By mixing different musical styles—from classical symphonies to popular jazz—Bernstein proved that theater music could achieve the highest level of artistic excellence while remaining accessible to the public.

9. With what topic is the first paragraph mainly concerned?
正解: B. How Bernstein expressed complex urban emotions and conflicts through his musical score
【解説】第1段落はバーンスタインが『ウエスト・サイド物語』において、ジャズのリズムやメロディを用いて都会の若者の複雑な感情（情熱、葛藤、失恋、緊張感と優しさ）をいかに表現したかが主眼となっています。A, C, Dは本文の単語を使っていますが焦点がずれています。
10. The word transformed in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to
正解: A. altered
【解説】`transformed`（姿・性質を変えた、変革した）の文脈上の同義語として最も適切なのは altered（〜を変容させた）です。B（保存した）、C（模倣した）、D（拡大した）は不適切です。
11. According to paragraph 1, listener experience in West Side Story is characterized by contrasting feelings of
正解: B. dramatic intensity and quiet, emotional warmth
【解説】第1段落末尾の「feel the tension in dramatic dance numbers as well as the gentle tenderness in romantic songs...」を言い換えた選択肢です。「緊張感（dramatic intensity）」と「優しさ（emotional warmth）」の対比を正しく捉えているBが正解です。
12. With what topic is the second paragraph mainly concerned?
正解: B. The tradition of combining classical music elements with popular theater and film
【解説】第2段落はバーンスタインだけでなく、ガーシュウィンやジョン・ウィリアムズらが「クラシック的な要素とポピュラーな舞台・映画を融合させた伝統」について述べています。C（拒絶した）やD（対立・ライバル関係）は本文の事実と反します。
13. The word This in paragraph 2 refers to
正解: A. the blending of orchestral music with dramatic storytelling in theater and film
【解説】`This powerful combination...` 直前の文章（ガーシュウィンやウィリアムズらが行った「交響楽と劇的ストーリーテリングの融合」）を指すAが最も包括的で適切です。
14. According to paragraph 1, Bernstein's score for West Side Story incorporates all of the following EXCEPT
正解: C. rigid classical opera forms
【解説】ジャズのリズム（A）、ロマンチックなメロディ（B）、緊迫したダンス（D）はすべて第1段落で描かれていますが、「厳格なクラシック・オペラ形式（rigid classical opera forms）」は組み込まれていないため、Cが正解です。
15. With what topic is the third paragraph mainly concerned?
正解: A. The artistic depth and social purpose behind theatrical music compositions
【解説】第3段落は、バーンスタインらの楽曲が単なるエンターテインメントにとどまらず、社会問題や人間の真実を表現する「芸術的深みと社会的意図」を持っていたことを解説しています。
16. The word They in paragraph 3 refers to
正解: C. Bernstein and other stage and screen composers
【解説】直前の文「Bernstein and other composers who wrote music for the stage and screen...」を受ける代名詞です。
17. All of the following are described as achievements of theatrical music in paragraph 3 EXCEPT
正解: C. replacing traditional classical concerts in modern American cities
【解説】A（社会テーマへの感情的深み）、B（高い芸術的基準の証明）、D（親しみやすさとスタイルの融合）は段落内に明記されています。Cの「伝統的クラシック演奏会を取って代わった（置換した）」という記述はどこにも無いため、これが正解です。
18. What is the main purpose of the passage?
正解: B. To explain how American composers elevated theatrical music by combining artistic tradition and popular culture
【解説】文章全体を通して、バーンスタインを中心にガーシュウィンやウィリアムズらが「芸術的伝統（クラシック等）」と「ポピュラー文化（ジャズ・映画・舞台）」を融合させ、劇場音楽の地位を高めたことを説明しています。
