ELTiS Practice Test

Grammar
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read some sentences and choose the best answer to complete each sentence.
1. Pure gold ________ at approximately 1,064 degrees Celsius.
正解: C. melts
【解説】不可算名詞（単数扱い）の Pure gold が主語で、科学的事実（不変の真理）を表すため、三人称単数現在形の melts（溶ける）が入ります。
2. Why did the museum directors plan ________ a new exhibit on renewable energy next month?
正解: A. to open
【解説】動詞 `plan` は目的語に to不定詞 をとるため、to open（開設すること）が正解です。
3. That modern solar oven was the most efficient device in ________ laboratory.
正解: D. their
【解説】後ろの名詞 laboratory を修飾する所有格 their（彼らの）が入ります。（A: ours は所有代名詞「私たちのもの」なので名詞の前に置けません）
4. Would you mind ________ the volume on the radio while I am taking this phone call?
正解: B. lowering
【解説】`Would you mind doing...?`（〜していただけますか）の表現。`mind` の目的語には動名詞をとるため lowering（下げること）が正解です。
5. The updated software process completed the calculations ________ than the previous version.
正解: D. more smoothly
【解説】動詞 completed を修飾する副詞 smoothly の比較級。接続詞 `than` があるため more smoothly が正解です。
6. Look at the bright stars ________ across the clear night sky above the observatory.
正解: B. shining
【解説】`Look at + 名詞 + 現在分詞`（〜が…しているのを見る）の形。名詞 the bright stars を後置修飾する現在分詞 shining（輝いている）が正解です。
7. ________ to discover a cleaner source of energy, the researchers worked tirelessly in the lab.
正解: A. Hoping
【解説】「〜することを望んで」という意味を作る分詞構文。現在分詞の Hoping が文頭に入ります。
8. If the team ________ the warning signs earlier, they could have prevented the equipment breakdown.
正解: C. had noticed
【解説】帰結節が `could have prevented`（仮定法過去完了：〜できただろうに）となっているため、If節内は `had + 過去分詞` の had noticed が正解です。
Reading and Comprehension
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read and answer questions about some passages.

1Modern kitchens are filled with automated devices designed to make daily food preparation faster and easier. Among these, the microwave oven is perhaps one of the most remarkable inventions of the twentieth century. Surprisingly, one was not created intentionally for cooking, but was instead discovered entirely by accident during military research. In 1945, an American engineer named Percy Spencer was working on active radar technology using a power tube called a magnetron. While standing near the operating machine, he noticed that a chocolate bar in his pocket had melted. Intrigued by this unusual reaction, Spencer decided to experiment further by placing corn kernels near the tube, which quickly popped into popcorn.

10Recognizing the massive commercial potential, Spencer's employer, the Raytheon Corporation, filed a patent for the microwave cooking process in 1945. The first commercial microwave oven, named the "Radarange," was introduced two years later. However, it was far from practical for home use. The unit stood nearly six feet tall, weighed over 750 pounds, and cost nearly $5,000—an enormous sum at the time. Furthermore, early models required expensive water cooling systems. Consequently, these giant machines were initially bought only by commercial venues such as restaurants, passenger ships, and military institutions that needed to heat large quantities of food rapidly.

18It took two more decades of technological advances to bring microwave ovens into average households. By the late 1960s, engineers had developed smaller, air-cooled magnetrons, enabling manufacturers to produce compact countertop models at affordable prices. As women increasingly joined the workforce and daily lifestyles accelerated, families emulated the trend toward quick-prep meals. By the late 1970s, the appliance had become a standard fixture in American homes. Today, the microwave oven symbolizes the shift toward modern convenience, fundamentally changing how food is stored, prepared, and enjoyed around the world.

9. What does this passage mainly discuss?
正解: B. The accidental discovery and evolution of the microwave oven.
【解説】文章全体を通して「電子レンジが偶然発見され、大型商用機から一般家庭に普及していく過程」を述べています。
10. In paragraph 1, the word one refers to
正解: C. a microwave oven
【解説】直前の文「...the microwave oven is perhaps one of the most remarkable inventions...」を受けて「驚くことに、（電子レンジは）調理用に作られたのではなかった」と続くため、a microwave oven を指します。
11. According to the passage, Percy Spencer first noticed the heating effect of the magnetron because
正解: D. a chocolate bar in his pocket melted near the machine
【解説】第1段落「he noticed that a chocolate bar in his pocket had melted」からDが正解です。
12. In paragraph 2, the word it refers to which of the following?
正解: A. The first commercial microwave oven
【解説】直前の「The first commercial microwave oven, named the 'Radarange'」を指しており、それが家庭用には実用的ではなかったと続いています。
13. According to paragraph 2, early microwave ovens were NOT suitable for regular home kitchens because they
正解: C. were extremely large, heavy, and expensive
【解説】第2段落「stood nearly six feet tall, weighed over 750 pounds, and cost nearly $5,000」の記述からCが当てはまります。
14. It can be inferred from paragraph 2 that the early "Radarange" was mainly purchased by
正解: B. businesses and organizations requiring large-scale food heating
【解説】第2段落文末「bought only by commercial venues such as restaurants, passenger ships, and military institutions...」からBが推論されます。
15. Why does the author mention "water cooling systems" in paragraph 2?
正解: A. To highlight another factor that made early microwaves impractical for homes.
【解説】「Furthermore...」に続いて「初期型が水冷システムを必要とし高価だった（＝家庭普及のハードルだった）」ことを補足するために言及されています。
16. Which of the following statements can best be inferred from the passage?
正解: D. Technological improvements in magnetrons helped lower the price and size of microwaves.
【解説】第3段落「engineers had developed smaller, air-cooled magnetrons, enabling manufacturers to produce compact countertop models at affordable prices」からDが推論できます。
17. In paragraph 3, the word emulated is closest in meaning to
正解: A. followed or adopted
【解説】`emulate`（真似る、見習う、倣う）に最も近い意味は followed or adopted です。
18. In paragraph 1, which noun means "a device or instrument used to detect distant objects using radio waves"?
正解: C. radar
【解説】「電波を使って遠方の物体を検知する装置」という定義に合致する名詞は第1段落の radar（レーダー）です。
