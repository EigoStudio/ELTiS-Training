ELTiS Practice Test

Grammar
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read some sentences and choose the best answer to complete each sentence.
1. The scientists working at the ancient archaeological site ________ excited about the new findings.
正解: B. are
【解説】主語は複数名詞の `The scientists`（修飾語句：working at the...）のため、複数主語の現在形に対応する be動詞 are が入ります。
2. ________ you know that ancient footprints were preserved in dry lakebeds?
正解: C. Do
【解説】一般動詞 `know` の二人称（you）に対する現在形の疑問文を作るため、Do が正解です。
3. The discovery of fossilized tracks often makes researchers ________ about early human behavior.
正解: A. wonder
【解説】使役動詞 `make + 目的語 + 原形不定詞` のパターンです。「〜に…させる」の意味となり、動詞の原形 wonder が正解です。
4. ________ these ancient seeds analyzed by geologists last year?
正解: A. Were
【解説】過去の受動態（were + 過去分詞）の疑問文です。複数主語 `these ancient seeds` に対応する Were が正解です。
5. The research team managed ________ the exact age of the layer using radiocarbon dating.
正解: D. to determine
【解説】動詞 `manage` は目的語に to不定詞 をとるため（manage to do：なんとか〜する）、to determine が正解です。
6. White Sands is the national park ________ contains well-preserved fossilized human footprints.
正解: C. which
【解説】空欄の後ろに動詞 `contains` が続いているため、主格の関係代名詞 which が正解です。（先行詞が場所であっても節内に主語がない場合は関係代名詞となります）
7. New Mexico is the state ________ researchers discovered evidence of early human presence.
正解: A. where
【解説】空欄の後ろに「主語＋動詞＋目的語（researchers discovered evidence...）」の完全な文が続いているため、場所を表す関係副詞 where が入ります。
8. If the seeds had not been preserved in the mud, archaeologists ________ the footprints so accurately.
正解: A. could not have dated
【解説】If節が `had not been preserved`（仮定法過去完了）となっているため、帰結節には `助動詞の過去形 + have + 過去分詞` の could not have dated が入ります。
Reading and Comprehension
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read and answer questions about some passages.

1For many decades, archaeologists generally agreed that the first human beings arrived in the Americas about 13,000 to 16,000 years ago, traveling across a land bridge connecting Asia and Alaska. However, a major archaeological discovery in New Mexico has challenged this long-held scientific consensus. At White Sands National Park, researchers uncovered dozens of fossilized human footprints embedded in the muddy layers of an ancient, dried-up lakebed.

8To determine when these footprints were made, scientists carefully analyzed tiny aquatic seeds trapped directly above and below the tracks in the sediment layers. Using advanced radiocarbon dating techniques, they determined that the footprints were made between 23,000 and 21,000 years ago. This means that humans were living in North America during the peak of the Last Glacial Maximum, thousands of years earlier than previously believed. The tracks reveal that teenagers and children frequently walked across the ancient lakebed, leaving behind clear physical records of their daily movements.

17This groundbreaking finding has forced experts to rethink their models regarding how and when early humans populated the Americas. The discovery proved that humans were already present long before melting ice sheets opened inland routes. Although they initially faced skepticism from some members of the scientific community, further studies confirmed the astonishing age of the sediment layers. Today, the White Sands site is recognized as one of the most pivotal archaeological locations in the Americas, opening an exciting new chapter in the study of early human history.

9. What does this passage mainly discuss?
正解: B. A discovery that changed beliefs about human arrival in the Americas
【解説】文章全体を通して、ホワイトサンズでの足跡発見がアメリカ大陸への人類到達の定説（1万3000〜1万6000年前）を覆し（約2万3000〜2万1000年前）、人類史の理解を大きく変えたことを解説しています。
10. In paragraph 1, the word consensus refers to
正解: A. general agreement among a group of people
【解説】直前の `archaeologists generally agreed` という記述に対応しており、`consensus` は「合意、一般的意見の一致」を意味します。
11. According to the passage, fossils formed from human tracks in White Sands were found in
正解: C. mud layers of a dried-up lakebed
【解説】第1段落末尾「...embedded in the muddy layers of an ancient, dried-up lakebed（乾いた古代の湖底の泥の地層に埋もれた）」の記述からCが正解です。
12. Which of the following statements can best be inferred from paragraph 2?
正解: B. The seeds helped establish a timeframe because they were in the same soil layers.
【解説】第2段落「...analyzed tiny aquatic seeds trapped directly above and below the tracks in the sediment layers... determined that the footprints were made between 23,000 and 21,000 years ago」から、同じ地層にあった種子が年代特定に役立ったことが推論されます。
13. In paragraph 3, the word pivotal is closest in meaning to
正解: C. crucial
【解説】`pivotal`（極めて重要な、中核となる）に最も近い意味を持つ形容詞は crucial（決定的な、極めて重要な）です。
14. Which of the following statements about the White Sands discovery is NOT true?
正解: C. The findings were immediately accepted by all scientists without any skepticism.
【解説】第3段落「Although they initially faced skepticism from some members of the scientific community...（当初一部の科学者から懐疑的な目で見られたものの）」と書かれており、Cの「全員に直ちに受け入れられた」は不適切（＝NOT true）です。
15. How did scientists find out the estimated age of the human footprints?
正解: B. By performing radiocarbon dating on organic seeds found in the sediment.
【解説】第2段落「...analyzed tiny aquatic seeds... Using advanced radiocarbon dating techniques...」よりBが正解です。
16. According to the passage, prior to this discovery, human arrival in the Americas was believed to be
正解: A. around 13,000 to 16,000 years ago
【解説】第1段落「...first human beings arrived in the Americas about 13,000 to 16,000 years ago...」よりAが正解です。
17. What is the most likely topic of the paragraph following this passage?
正解: A. Further research and new excavations being conducted at White Sands.
【解説】最終段落末尾が「ホワイトサンズは人類史研究の刺激的な新しい章を開いている」と締めくくられているため、続く展開としては「さらなる調査やホワイトサンズでの新しい発掘作業」に関するトピック（A）が最も自然です。
18. In paragraph 3, the word they refers to which of the following?
正解: C. The research findings / researchers
【解説】第3段落「Although they initially faced skepticism...（当初懐疑論に直面したものの）」の `they` は、研究結果（または研究チーム）を指しています。
