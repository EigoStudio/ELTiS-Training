ELTiS Practice Test

Grammar
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read some sentences and choose the best answer to complete each sentence.
11. Protecting wild animals in their natural habitats ________ necessary for preserving biodiversity.
正解: C. is
【解説】主語は動名詞フレーズ「Protecting wild animals...（野生動物を保護すること）」で単数扱いのため、述語動詞には is が入ります。
12. ________ the researchers studying the behavior of giant pandas in the mountains?
正解: A. Are
【解説】複数主語「the researchers」＋現在分詞「studying」が続いているため、現在進行形の疑問文を作る Are が正解です。
13. The zoologist watched the mother panda carry ________ cub carefully across the stream.
正解: D. her
【解説】単数メスの名詞「the mother panda」を修飾する所有格の her が入ります。
14. I ________ to that famous national park twice before to see rare birds.
正解: B. have been
【解説】「twice before（以前に2度）」から「経験」を表す現在完了形 have been (to...) が正解です。
15. The government kept the wildlife reserve ________ to protect the endangered species.
正解: A. closed
【解説】`keep + O + 過去分詞` で「Oを〜された状態に保つ」。「閉鎖される」受動の関係のため過去分詞 closed が正解です。
16. ________ the weather was harsh in the high mountains, the scientists did not cancel their field study.
正解: C. Although
【解説】後ろに節（主語＋動詞：the weather was harsh）が続いているため、接続詞の Although（〜だけれども）が入ります。Despiteは前置詞のため節を導けません。
17. There was no easy way to track the elusive animals in the dense forest, so the team gave up ________ them until spring.
正解: B. searching
【解説】熟語 `give up doing`（〜することを諦める/やめる）の形をとるため、動名詞の searching が入ります。
18. This semester's biology exam was ________ than last semester's one.
正解: D. easier
【解説】後ろに接続詞 `than` があるため比較級が入ります。easyの比較級は easier です。
Reading and Comprehension
Directions: In this part of the test, you will read and answer questions about some passages.

1For decades, giant pandas have served as unique international ambassadors for China. Under a political strategy known as "panda diplomacy," the Chinese government loans these rare animals to foreign zoos as a symbol of friendship, goodwill, and trade partnerships. Thousands of visitors line up at zoos around the world each year to catch a glimpse of the iconic black-and-white bears. They are instructed not to make loud noises or use flash photography near the enclosures. For foreign cities, housing a giant panda brings massive public excitement and economic profit; it also strengthens diplomatic ties with Beijing. However, renting pandas is not free. Foreign zoos must pay an annual fee of up to one million dollars per pair, and any cubs born overseas remain the legal property of China and must be returned after a few years according to the posted rules.

10The environmental impact of panda diplomacy, however, remains a subject of debate. Wildlife conservationists point out that the high rental fees paid by foreign institutions are restricted to funding wild panda protection and habitat restoration inside China. Through these funds, China has built dozens of natural reserves, which helped upgrade the giant panda's status from "endangered" to "vulnerable" on global wildlife lists. Like other charismatic megafauna, giant pandas act as an "umbrella species"—protecting their bamboo forest habitat automatically saves countless smaller mammals, birds, and insects sharing the same ecosystem.

18Because of the giant panda's political sensitivity and complex biology, keeping them in captivity requires specialized care and controversy often arises. Political activists and animal welfare groups have sometimes criticized panda loans. Some argue that long-distance transport and unfamiliar climates subject the bears to severe stress—during past political tensions between countries, loan agreements have even been canceled or delayed. Some critics go as far as calling panda diplomacy an unethical exploitation of wildlife for political leverage, arguing that these gentle creatures should live freely in the wild rather than being used as diplomatic instruments.

19. Which of the following can best be inferred from the passage?
正解: B. Political disputes between nations can affect international panda loan agreements.
【解説】第3段落の「during past political tensions... loan agreements have even been canceled or delayed（国家間の政治的緊張の際、貸与協定が取消・遅延された）」から、政治的対立がパンダの貸与協定に影響を与えることが推論できます。
20. In paragraph 1, the word They refers to which of the following?
正解: C. Visitors
【解説】直前の文「Thousands of visitors line up...（何千もの来訪者が並ぶ）」を受けて、「（来訪者たちは）大きな声を出したりフラッシュ撮影をしないよう指導される」となるため、Visitors を指します。
21. Which of the following do visitors at foreign zoos NOT do, according to the passage?
正解: C. Take flash photos near panda enclosures
【解説】第1段落に「instructed not to ... use flash photography（フラッシュ撮影をしないよう指導される）」と記載されているため、C（フラッシュ撮影をすること）は「行わないこと」に該当します。
22. In paragraph 1, the word it refers to which of the following?
正解: A. Housing a giant panda
【解説】前文の動名詞句「housing a giant panda（ジャイアントパンダを飼育・寄託すること）」が、経済的利益をもたらすだけでなく、外交関係を強化すること（it）を指しています。
23. In paragraph 2, the word restricted is closest in meaning to
正解: C. limited
【解説】「restricted（〜に制限されている/限定されている）」に最も近い意味を持つ単語は limited です。
24. According to the passage, giant pandas are described as an "umbrella species" because protecting them
正解: B. helps preserve other wild animals sharing the same environment
【解説】第2段落文末の「protecting their bamboo forest habitat automatically saves countless smaller mammals...（竹林の生息地を守ることが自動的に同じ生態系を共有する他の動物を救う）」からBが正解です。
25. The rental fees paid by foreign zoos have helped China do all of the following EXCEPT
正解: C. pay for long-distance transport of other animals
【解説】第2段落に記載されている資金使途に含まれないものはC「他の動物の長距離輸送の費用を払うこと」です。
26. According to the passage, some animal welfare activists believe that
正解: D. pandas should live freely in the wild rather than being leased
【解説】第3段落最後の文「arguing that these gentle creatures should live freely in the wild rather than being used as diplomatic instruments（外交手段として使われるより野生で自由に生きるべきと主張する）」からDが正解です。
27. In paragraph 1, which noun means "a country's official representative sent to a foreign nation"?
正解: B. ambassador
【解説】「他国に派遣される国の公式な代表者」という定義に当てはまる名詞は第1段落1行目の ambassador（大使、使節）です。
28. What is the main idea of paragraph 2?
正解: A. Panda diplomacy has helped fund environmental conservation and habitat protection.
【解説】第2段落は、パンダ外交によって得られたレンタル料が中国国内の環境保護区の設立や野生パンダの保護活動資金に役立っているという環境的側面を述べています。
